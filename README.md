# Welcome to Your Personal Health & Wellness Dashboard! 🎉
This fantastic app is designed to be your go-to tool for tracking your health journey. It's split into three super useful tabs:

- BMI Calculator: To check your Body Mass Index and track it over time.
- Daily Metabolic Rate: To figure out exactly how many calories your body needs each day.
- Food Tracker: To log your meals and see how you're stacking up against your goals!
---

# Tab 1: The BMI Calculator! 💪
First up, let's calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI). It's a great way to get a snapshot of your body composition.

How to use:
- Enter Your Height: Pop your height into the "Height" box. Don't forget to select whether you're using cm or inches!
- Enter Your Weight: Next, type your weight into the "Weight" box and choose kg or lbs.
- (Optional) Add a Date: If you want to track your progress (which is a super cool feature!), enter the current date in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2025-10-15).
- Hit that "Calculate BMI" Button! 💥

What You'll See:
- BMI Value: Your calculated BMI will appear instantly!
- BMI Category: The app will tell you which category you fall into, like "Normal weight" or "Overweight."
- BMI Tracking Chart: If you entered a date, you'll see a point appear on the graph. Keep coming back to add more points and watch your progress unfold over time!

---

# Tab 2: Daily Metabolic Rate! 🔥
This is where the magic happens! We'll figure out your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). In simple terms: we're calculating the fuel your body needs just to live, and how much it needs with your daily activities included!

How to use:
- Enter Your Details: Fill in your Gender, Age (in years), Height (in cm), and Weight (in kg).
- Choose Your Activity Level: Be honest here! Select how active you are from the dropdown menu. The info text gives you a handy guide.
- Click "Calculate My TDEE & Goals"! ✨

What You'll See:
- BMR: The number of Calories your body burns at complete rest.
- TDEE: This is the BIG one! It's the total number of Calories your body burns in a day, including exercise. Remember this number for the next tab!
- Calorie Goals: The app automatically gives you suggested daily Calorie targets for maintaining, losing, or gaining weight. Awesome!

---

# Tab 3: The Food Tracker! 🥗
Now it's time to put that TDEE number to work! Track what you eat to see if you're hitting your Calorie goals.

How to use:
- IMPORTANT: You need to calculate your TDEE in the "Daily Metabolic Rate" tab first for this tab to work best!
- Check Your TDEE: Your TDEE from the previous tab is displayed at the top for easy reference.
- Log Your Calories: Every time you eat something, just type the number of Calories into the "enter Calories" box.
- Hit the "Log Food" Button!

What You'll See:
- Daily Calorie Total: See your total Calories for the day add up with each entry.
- Calorie Difference: This shows you if you're over or under your TDEE for the day.
- Calorie Intake Chart: This brilliant bar chart shows your daily calorie intake for the last 7 days. The yellow dashed line is your TDEE, so you can see at a glance how you're doing!
